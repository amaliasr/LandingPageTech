A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT ASK

Before we say hello.

I have an idea, but no technical background. Is that okay?

Of course. Tell us what you want to make easier or what you’d like to build. We’ll help you figure out what the software needs to do and explain the options in plain language.

Do you only work in the industries shown here?

No. Those products show a few of the problems we work on. If your business looks different, we’d still like to hear what you need.

How much will a project cost, and how long will it take?

That depends on the scope, the features, and what you already have. We’ll talk through the work with you before putting together an estimate. There’s no one-size-fits-all package.

Can we start small and add more later?

Yes. We can focus on the part that matters most first, then plan the next steps around what you learn from using it.
