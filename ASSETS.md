# Asset provenance

The brand and portrait assets were provided or commissioned by the owner of R Studio Hub. The original portrait is not included in this repository.

- `public/assets/logo-r.png`: the approved purple R monogram from the R Studio Hub identity project, with an alpha-transparent background.
- `public/assets/owner-portrait.png`: user-provided portrait with only the background removed through built-in ImageGen.
- `public/assets/hub-orbit.png`: original decorative hero artwork generated with built-in ImageGen.

## Hero artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Original premium software company landing-page hero artwork, wide landscape 16:9. A single enormous sculptural liquid-metal toroidal halo, oval circular open loop with a deep dark open center, viewed at a front three-quarter angle. Seamless dark charcoal almost-black background in the visual family of #0c0b11. Highly polished dark chrome with pale lavender iridescent rim reflections and subtle silver highlights; physically believable reflective metal and smooth contours. Centered in the bottom two-thirds with the full silhouette visible, generous near-black negative space above and in corners. Loop plane tilted slightly backward with subtle rightward rotation. Restrained violet glow around lower rim, pale lavender studio rim lighting, quiet cinematic product-render aesthetic. Palette: almost black, dark chrome, silver, pale lavender, restrained violet. Opaque background. No text, letters, symbols, logos, watermark, stars, space scene, planets, particles, UI cards, gold, busy geometry, or extra objects. Website typography and cards will be native HTML outside the image.

## Portrait edit prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent Owner portrait for R Studio Hub landing page. Input image: the supplied portrait is the edit target. Remove only the plain white background to true alpha transparency. Preserve the foreground person exactly, with the original crop and framing. Keep the exact identity, face, facial proportions, expression, gaze, pose, skin tone, skin texture, natural lighting, black hijab, black blazer, and all clothing details unchanged. Clean accurate edges around the hijab and shoulders. Do not beautify, retouch the face, restyle, change attire, or recreate the subject. No new background, no added shadow, no text, no watermark. Transparent pixels must have a real alpha channel.
